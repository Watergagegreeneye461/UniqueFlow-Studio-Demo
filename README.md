Welcome to UniqueFlow-Studio-Demo, a powerful video editor designed for Windows users who want to create short-form clips quickly and easily. Whether you're making content for social media, music videos, or fast creative projects, this desktop app focuses on local-first processing—meaning everything runs on your computer without cloud dependencies. UniqueFlow-Studio-Demo is an ideal tool for content creators who need automatic video editing for short-form videos. Use it to:

## 📖 How to Use

1. **Download the app** using the link above.
2. **Run the installer** to get started.
3. **Open UniqueFlow-Studio-Demo** from your Start menu.
4. **Import your video clip** by clicking the Import button.
5. **Select an automatic editing preset** for short-form output.
6. **Add a music track** from your computer.
7. **Preview** the result.
8. **Export** your video to share with the world.

## ⚙️ Tips for Best Performance

- Close other apps while editing heavy videos.
- Use clips under 5 minutes for best performance.
- Keep source files in a folder you can find easily.
- Use MP4 format for smoother experience.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

- **App doesn’t start:** Ensure you have latest Windows updates installed.
- **Slow performance:** Close background apps and low-end hardware.
- **FFmpeg errors:** Reinstall the app (FFmpeg is bundled automatically).
- **Cannot import video:** Try a common format like MP4 or AVI.
